About this tracker

This is a private, no-login tracker: your holdings are saved in your browser's local storage, never sent to a server, so only you can see them. Prices come from the same daily market data the rest of the site uses, so values refresh roughly once a day rather than tick by tick. It's meant to give you a quick, honest picture of where your money sits and how it's split - not a brokerage statement.

Educational only, not investment advice. Prices are scraped daily and may lag or differ from your broker. Cash and savings are shown at face value (no profit accrual). Verify exact holdings and values with your broker, bank and CDC account.
