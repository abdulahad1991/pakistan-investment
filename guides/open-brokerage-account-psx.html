How to open a brokerage account for PSX.

01The three pieces of a PSX account, and why the separation matters

A retail setup connects an execution relationship, a securities record and a payment account. They are related, but their controls and complaint routes differ.

First, the brokerage account

This is the contractual relationship through which orders are entered. A firm must hold a PSX Trading Right Entitlement Certificate and the relevant SECP securities-broker licence. The live PSX broker directory shows status and category; check for Active rather than relying on a name, advertisement or old list.

Second, the CDC sub-account

Eligible securities are recorded in electronic book-entry form at the Central Depository Company. A CDC sub-account sits under a participant and is operated through that participant. A CDC Investor Account is operated directly under CDC's Investor Account Services. These are not identical custody arrangements, so read the current CDC terms and monitor the record independently.

Third, your bank account

The account-opening record includes a bank account or IBAN for permitted transfers and cash benefits. Confirm the accepted funding instructions in the signed form. Do not send money to a salesperson's personal account or to payment details received only through a message.

Your Unique Identification Number (UIN) links activity across the clearing system. After opening, verify the UIN and CDC account details shown in the broker's confirmation rather than treating an app login as proof that the regulated setup is complete.

02Choosing a broker: what actually matters

Start with the PSX TREC directory, not a site's affiliate list or popularity ranking. PSX publishes the broker's current status and operating category. The directory also links the separate list of firms offering online opening. Verify the exact legal name in both the directory and the account-opening agreement.

Request the same documents from each candidate: customer relationship form, risk disclosure, tariff or commission schedule, minimum-charge rules, custody and clearing charges, cash-withdrawal procedure, complaint escalation path, and the broker's policy for inactive accounts. Use a sample trade value to calculate total buy and sell costs; a headline commission percentage may omit minimums and pass-through charges.

Operational checks include two-factor authentication, contract-note delivery, withdrawal approval controls, outage support and the ability to reconcile positions against CDC. Research reports and app design are service features, not evidence of solvency, licensing status or suitability.

03Sahulat and standard accounts

A Sahulat Account uses simplified due diligence for an eligible resident individual and restricts the available markets. As of the SECP change announced 14 March 2026, the purchase-position limit is PKR 3 million per brokerage account. An investor may hold Sahulat accounts with multiple licensed brokers, but only one with each broker.

The current PSX Sahulat page limits trading to the Ready Market and restricts leveraged products, stock lending and borrowing, and negotiated deals. It also states that the account can be converted to a normal trading account.

A standard account uses the fuller KYC and source-of-funds process and is governed by a different customer relationship form. Compare the current forms on the PSX account-opening page. Do not select an account merely because it has fewer fields; match its market permissions and controls to the transactions you expect to make.

04Step-by-step: opening your account online

PSX supports online opening, but the exact verification route and document set depend on the account type, broker category and customer. Use this sequence to audit the process rather than treating it as a guaranteed timeline.

1

Shortlist and verify your broker

Confirm the firm's exact legal name, TREC number, Active status and category in the PSX directory. Open the application from the broker URL shown in an official source, not from a forwarded payment or signup link.

2

Fill the online form with your CNIC

Match the form title to the broker category and to conventional or Shariah-compliant opening. Supply the identity and KYC information requested in that form. Sahulat uses simplified due diligence, but it is still subject to the broker's risk assessment and regulatory checks.

3

Verify your identity against NADRA

Complete the identity, mobile, bank-account and other checks presented through the broker's official process. Verification may use the Centralized Gateway, Shared KYC or another permitted route. Record any reference number and do not disclose an OTP to an agent.

4

Add your IBAN and sign the declarations

Check the registered IBAN, tax residency, Zakat treatment, nominee or joint-holder fields where applicable, communication preferences, and risk disclosure. A CZ-50 is a prescribed declaration used only where its legal conditions apply; it is not a generic checkbox for every applicant.

5

Verify activation records

Wait for the broker's formal confirmation, then verify the assigned UIN, CDC account type and account number, registered contact details, tariff and contract-note channel. Processing time varies; an app login alone does not confirm every market-infrastructure record.

6

Fund the account from your own bank

Use only the permitted bank details and funding method stated in the signed agreement or verified broker portal. Reconcile the first credit in the broker ledger before entering an order. Never pay an employee, introducer or social-media group personally.

05CDC: sub-account, investor account, and how to verify your shares yourself

A CDC sub-account is maintained under a participant, which operates it for settlement under the applicable mandate. A CDC Investor Account is opened through Investor Account Services and has different operating controls. PSX's investor guide encourages investors to consider an Investor Account for greater individual custody, but the choice affects transfer and trading workflow. Read CDC's current fees, terms and transaction controls before choosing.

Enable the official CDC access and alert facilities available for the account. Reconcile security name, quantity and every movement against broker contract notes. A mismatch, unexplained debit or missing confirmation should be raised promptly through the broker's documented complaint route and, if unresolved, the PSX/SECP escalation channels. Separate records reduce detection time; they do not justify a guarantee that loss is impossible.

06Placing your first order

A market order seeks execution at available prices and does not fix the final price. A limit order sets a maximum purchase price or minimum sale price but may remain unfilled. Before submission, record the symbol, side, quantity, market, order type, limit where relevant, estimated charges and validity. The broker's confirmation should show what actually executed.

PSX separates the Ready Market and Odd Lot Market, and marketable quantities depend on the market and security. The PSX product page states that the Odd Lot Market supports quantities below the normal lot, with a minimum of one share. Check the security's current market, lot and price in the official trading interface; old share-price examples are not useful account-opening evidence.

Eligible PSX trades moved to a T+1 settlement cycle from 9 February 2026. A trade on business day T is scheduled for settlement on the next business day, subject to the market calendar and applicable rules. This replaced T+2; see the PSX transition notice.

When a listed company declares a dividend, it announces a book-closure period and the exchange publishes the associated ex-dividend date. Eligible cash dividends, net of withholding, are paid through the account details registered against the holding. Yields change with both distributions and share prices; use the current PSX dividend screen as a starting dataset, then verify each distribution in the company's PSX notices.

07What it all costs

Build a cost sheet from the broker's written tariff: commission and any minimum per order, PSX/NCCPL/CDC pass-through charges, sales tax or other levies, bank-transfer costs and withdrawal charges. Calculate the round-trip cost at the intended order size. Do not compare brokers using a single advertised commission line.

Tax is separate from transaction fees. Listed-share capital gains and dividend withholding can depend on acquisition date, security or distribution classification, ATL status and the current law. NCCPL performs central functions in the CGT regime, but that does not eliminate a taxpayer's return and recordkeeping obligations. Use the FBR Tax Year 2027 rate card, the current Ordinance and NCCPL statements for the actual tax year. The investment-tax guide explains the evidence trail without substituting a universal rate.

08Safety: broker default and the scams to avoid

A CDC record and client-asset rules provide important controls, but they are not a promise that every balance will be recovered immediately after fraud, operational failure or broker default. Securities and cash follow different records and claim processes. Retain account forms, bank proofs, contract notes, CDC statements and withdrawal requests so a claim can be evidenced. Use the complaint process in the PSX investor guide and escalate unresolved regulated-entity complaints through official channels.

Verification test: match the entity's legal name and Active status in the PSX directory; obtain the prescribed account form and tariff; use only verified institutional payment details; confirm the UIN and CDC record; and reconcile each trade to a contract note. Guaranteed returns, personal-account deposits, remote-access requests, unexplained OTP requests and an entity absent from the directory are stop signals.

09Pre-trade control checklist

  • Order evidence: save the order and contract note; compare symbol, side, quantity, price, charges and settlement date.
  • Liquidity: inspect bid/ask depth and recent volume; a low nominal share price does not establish value or ease of exit.
  • Leverage: read margin, mark-to-market and forced-sale terms before enabling any leveraged market; Sahulat accounts restrict these products.
  • Information: trace a claim to a PSX company filing or other primary record; do not treat a group message, target price or promised return as evidence.
  • Reconciliation: compare the broker ledger, bank movement, NCCPL tax record and CDC securities record at a regular interval.

10Frequently asked questions

Is there one minimum amount for every PSX account?
No single figure applies across brokers, account types, securities and market lots. Check the broker's opening and funding minimum, the security's current lot and price, and the minimum commission. The Odd Lot Market supports quantities below a normal lot, with PSX stating a minimum of one share.
Does a CDC sub-account eliminate broker-default risk?
No. Depository records and client-asset rules are important controls, but securities, cash and disputed movements can follow different recovery processes. Verify holdings independently and retain the account agreement, bank evidence, contract notes, CDC statements and complaint records.
What is the current Sahulat Account limit?
SECP raised the purchase-position limit to PKR 3 million per brokerage account in March 2026. The account uses simplified due diligence and is limited to the Ready Market; leveraged products, stock lending and borrowing, and negotiated deals are restricted. Recheck the live PSX page before applying.
How do I verify a cash dividend?
Start with the company's PSX distribution and book-closure notices, then reconcile the eligible holding against the CDC record, the registered payment details, the bank credit and the withholding certificate. Use the current FBR rate card for the payment year.
Can a PSX brokerage account be opened online?
PSX has an online-account-opening framework and publishes a list of participating firms. Availability, verification route, documents and processing time depend on the broker and applicant. Complete the process only through the verified firm or permitted shared-KYC channel.
Which account-opening documents control?
Use the current PSX form that matches the broker category, account type and conventional or Shariah-compliant route. Identity, KYC, bank, tax-residency and source-of-funds requirements vary by route. Keep a completed copy and the tariff.
What is the PSX route for a non-resident Pakistani?
PSX documents the Roshan Equity Investment route for eligible Roshan Digital Account holders, including its current bank and broker lists. Non-resident onboarding, funding, repatriation and tax records differ from a resident Sahulat account, so follow the current RDA/REI workflow rather than this resident-account summary.
How should broker charges be compared?
Obtain the written commission and tariff schedule, including minimum per order and PSX, NCCPL, CDC and tax pass-throughs. Calculate a buy and sale at the same sample order value for each broker. Keep tax rates separate from service fees.
Where should current PSX tax rates be checked?
Use FBR's Tax Year 2027 withholding rate card and the current Income Tax Ordinance, then reconcile NCCPL and dividend-withholding records. The relevant rate can depend on the transaction, security, acquisition date and ATL status.
What proves that the account is active?
Use the broker's formal confirmation plus the assigned UIN, CDC account details and registered communication records. Processing time varies, and receiving an app password does not by itself prove that every record is complete.
