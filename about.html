What Pakistan Investment Advisor Covers

Most Pakistani investors are overwhelmed by choice — National Savings certificates, dozens of mutual funds, hundreds of stocks. Our tool cuts through the noise by comparing the most important options side by side, personalised to your budget.

🏦 National Savings Schemes (CDNS)

We track all major schemes offered by the Central Directorate of National Savings, including Special Savings Certificates, Defence Savings Certificates, Regular Income Certificates, and Bahbood Savings Certificates. Rates are sourced from savings.gov.pk and updated as CDNS publishes new profit rates.

📊 Mutual Funds (SECP-Regulated)

We cover the top-performing mutual funds in Pakistan, with a focus on both Islamic (Shariah-compliant) and conventional funds. Return data is sourced from MUFAP (Mutual Funds Association of Pakistan) and fund houses' official websites. We include 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year annualised returns to give you a complete picture.

📈 PSX Blue-Chip Stocks

We track the highest dividend-paying companies on the Pakistan Stock Exchange, sorted by dividend yield. Stock prices are updated daily from PSX data. We focus on KSE-100 companies with long track records of consistent dividend payments — the most suitable stocks for income-focused investors.

How This Site Gets Built (A Confession) 🤝

Full disclosure: Pakistan Investment Advisor is built by a two-member pair-programming team. One half is Abdul Ahad — human, salaried, has actually felt the sting of a 35% tax slab and stood in a National Savings queue. The other half is Claude — an AI that has never paid withholding tax, never queued for anything, and holds strong opinions about em-dashes.

The division of labour, roughly:

AA
Abdul brings
The lived experience, the Pakistani context an AI keeps getting subtly wrong, the chai breaks, and the final veto on anything that reads like a robot wrote it. Also decides which numbers actually matter to a family in Lahore — not a textbook.
🤖
Claude brings
Tireless typing at 3am with no demand for tea, the patience to hedge every single rate back to its official source, and an enthusiasm for tables that occasionally has to be talked down. Does not, contrary to rumour, get to publish anything without Abdul reading it first.

The house rules of the pairing:

  • Every rate is checked against savings.gov.pk, sbp.org.pk or fbr.gov.pk — the AI is confident, which is precisely why a human verifies it.
  • If a sentence sounds like it's entering a vocabulary contest, it gets cut.
  • Abdul holds the only key to the "publish" button. Claude has lobbying rights only.
  • Arguments about the Oxford comma are settled by whoever pays the hosting bill. (It's Abdul.)

The serious bit: AI helps us ship more guides, faster, and keep them current — but a real person with real skin in the Pakistani market signs off on every word. The maths is double-checked; the judgement stays human.
