How gold pricing works in Pakistan

Gold trades globally in US dollars per troy ounce (31.1035 g) of pure 24-karat gold. Pakistan quotes gold in rupees per tola (11.6638 g), and also per 10 grams and per gram. Because two variables drive the rupee price - the global gold price and the exchange rate - Pakistani gold can rise even when international gold is flat, simply because the rupee weakened. Purity matters: 22K ≈ 91.7% of 24K, 21K ≈ 87.5%, 18K ≈ 75%. The daily reference is published by the Sarafa associations; the shop price also adds making charges.

What a gold comparison must include

Gold produces no interest or dividend. A fair comparison therefore includes the buy/sell spread, jewellery making charges, purity testing, storage, liquidity, zakat and the possibility of long price declines. Historical rupee appreciation alone cannot determine whether gold fits a person's time horizon or risk capacity. The gold guide explains these checks without prescribing an allocation.

Zakat note: 2.5% is a commonly used rate after the applicable nisab and lunar-year conditions are met, but treatment can differ by school and circumstances. The zakat calculator is arithmetic, not a religious ruling.

FAQ

Gold rate questions

Is the displayed rate a live dealer quote?
No. The table is the latest successfully collected third-party reference and shows its date and source above. A dealer's quote can differ because of timing, city, spread, purity and making charges.
How is the gold price calculated?
Gold is priced internationally in US dollars per troy ounce of 24K gold. The rupee price per tola moves with both the global gold price and the PKR/USD exchange rate. One tola is 11.6638 grams; 22K is about 91.7% of the 24K rate.
Does the scenario calculator predict gold prices?
No. It applies the constant annual percentage entered by the user. The default is 0%, and the result excludes spreads, charges, storage, tax and zakat.
What differs between 24K and 22K gold?
24K is nominally pure gold, while 22K contains about 91.7% gold and other metals for durability. Actual products also differ in certification, workmanship, dealer spread and resale terms.
How is zakat on gold assessed?
A commonly used calculation is 2.5% after applicable nisab and lunar-year conditions are met. Treatment can vary by school and personal circumstances, so the site's calculator should not be treated as a religious ruling.
