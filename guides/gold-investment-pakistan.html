Gold investment in Pakistan: physical, digital & funds.

By Abdul Ahad · Source-checked 21 August 2026 · 11 min read
Three ways to own gold

Physical bullion, regulated commodity funds and PMEX contracts create different ownership, custody, leverage, fee and liquidity exposures. None provides a guaranteed hedge or return.

Three structures: physical bullion creates purity, spread and custody risk; commodity funds issue units at NAV and charge expenses; PMEX contracts follow exchange specifications and may use leverage. Compare the exact instrument rather than treating all three as equivalent gold ownership.

01Why Pakistanis Hold Gold: The Rupee Story

A rupee gold quote is influenced by the international gold price, the PKR/USD conversion and local market premiums. For a reproducible calculation, record the international spot observation, the SBP exchange-rate observation, unit conversion and the dealer's executable quote at the same time.

A weaker rupee can raise the local price even when dollar gold is unchanged, but local premiums, spreads and the dollar gold price can offset or amplify that effect. Past protection during one currency episode is not proof that gold will preserve purchasing power over every holding period.

Do not compare the policy rate directly with gold performance. Use the actual after-fee and after-tax return of the alternative product and a matched CPI period from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics. The policy-rate/CPI gap is not a saver’s realised return.

02How Gold Is Priced in Pakistan: Tola, Karat, and the Sarafa Rate

Before comparing quotes, standardise the units. One tola is 11.6638 grams. Dealers may quote per tola, 10 grams or gram, while international markets commonly quote US dollars per troy ounce. Record the local executable quote and compare its implied price with the same-time international observation and SBP exchange-rate data. Local market data on this site may use a disclosed fallback, so check its source label.

Purity is measured in karats. 24-karat gold is 99.9% pure and is what bars and coins are made of. 22-karat is about 91.6% pure, alloyed with copper or silver for durability, and it is the standard for most Pakistani jewellery, with 21k and 18k also common. The quoted sarafa rate is for pure gold. A 22k item contains proportionally less, and an honest jeweller prices it accordingly.

The third concept is making charges (and their cousin, "wastage"). Jewellery costs the gold value plus a craftsmanship charge that ranges from a small percentage to well over a tenth of the item's value for intricate designs. When you sell it back, the jeweller pays for the gold content only. The making charges are gone, and many shops deduct further wastage on top. That round trip is why jewellery is a gift and an heirloom, but a poor investment vehicle.

03The Three Ways to Own Gold in Pakistan

Physical Gold
Bars, coins, or jewellery from a jeweller. Tangible and universally accepted - but you carry storage, theft, and purity risk plus a buy-sell spread.
Gold Mutual Funds
Regulated commodity-scheme units with portfolio, NAV, expense, custody and redemption terms defined by fund documents.
PMEX Gold Contracts
Exchange contracts whose size, margin, daily settlement and delivery rules are defined by PMEX specifications.

Option 1: Physical Gold - Bars, Coins, and Jewellery

Physical bullion and jewellery have different purposes and costs. Compare the stated fineness, verified weight, hallmark or assay evidence, making charge, dealer premium and written buy-back deduction. A detailed receipt should identify the item, weight, fineness, rate, charges, date and seller.

Storage creates theft, access, documentation and insurance questions. A locker fee does not by itself establish insurance for its contents. The buy/sell spread is dealer- and item-specific, so obtain both an executable purchase quote and a same-time buy-back quote instead of assuming a fixed percentage.

Option 2: Gold-Backed Mutual Funds - The Digital Route

SECP-regulated asset managers may offer commodity schemes with gold exposure. Use MUFAP's category data to identify current schemes, then read each offering document and factsheet for holdings, benchmark, Shariah governance where relevant, custody, expenses and redemption terms.

A fund removes personal custody of physical metal but adds fund-level market, tracking, counterparty, operational, fee and redemption risks. Minimums and settlement schedules vary. Compare a fund's NAV performance with its stated benchmark over the same dates and disclose any stale MUFAP observation rather than ranking providers.

Option 3: PMEX Gold Contracts - Handle With Care

The Pakistan Mercantile Exchange (PMEX) is the country's SECP-regulated commodity futures exchange. It lists gold contracts in a range of sizes, from one-tola and gram-denominated contracts up to ounce-based contracts tied to international prices, traded through PMEX-licensed brokers (the exchange publishes the list).

Some PMEX contracts use margin and daily mark-to-market, which can create losses larger than the initial cash margin and margin-call or forced-close risk. Contract size, expiry, delivery and settlement differ. Read the current PMEX specification and broker risk disclosure; an exchange contract is not a savings account or the same legal exposure as allocated physical metal.

04Gold vs income assets: match the inputs

Unleased physical gold pays no coupon or dividend. Its realised return depends on purchase price, sale price, spreads, custody and tax. Income assets use different cash flows and risks, so compare them over the same dates and on the same net basis.

Any historical comparison must specify start and end dates, reinvested distributions, transaction costs and taxes. Results are sensitive to the chosen window. Calling gold “insurance” describes an intended role, not a contractual payout or guaranteed correlation during a loss event.

Key insight

Use actual product cash flows and gold buy/sell quotes. Subtract fees and applicable tax, then compare both results with CPI over the same period. A policy rate or headline certificate rate is not a realised real return, and a sovereign product is not free of every risk.

05Allocation questions, not a fixed percentage

No primary source used here establishes a universal gold percentage. The answer depends on existing jewellery or bullion, currency exposure, income needs, time horizon, storage capacity, loss tolerance and the role assigned to gold in a broader plan.

Inventory existing holdings at verifiable resale value, not purchase cost. Then stress-test a sharp gold decline, a wider local spread, a stable rupee and a period in which income assets outperform. This page provides no model portfolio or provider preference.

06Due-diligence checklist by structure

Physical gold

  1. Check the day's sarafa rate before visiting the shop, so you know the fair benchmark.
  2. Choose an established, certified jeweller - ideally one that will buy back at a stated rate.
  3. Record the intended use and compare bullion with jewellery on fineness, making charges and resale deductions.
  4. Watch the item being weighed on a digital scale, and confirm the karat marking yourself.
  5. Take a detailed receipt - weight, karat, rate, date, total - and store it separately from the gold.
  6. Document custody, access, insurance terms and recovery evidence before taking possession.

Gold mutual fund

  1. Identify commodity schemes in MUFAP data and confirm the AMC in the SECP directory.
  2. Read the offering document, factsheet, benchmark, holdings, expense ratio, loads and risk profile.
  3. Confirm the applicable NAV, cut-off, minimum and account-specific KYC requirements.
  4. Record the formal redemption schedule and bank-account rules; do not rely on a generic settlement estimate.

PMEX gold contracts

  1. Read the exact contract specification, expiry, delivery, margin and daily mark-to-market rules.
  2. Verify the intermediary on the current list at pmex.com.pk and use only official payment instructions.
  3. Model adverse price moves, margin calls, forced closure, fees and settlement before entering any order.

07The Risks, Plainly

  • Spread and round-trip costs: you buy at a premium and sell at a discount. Jewellery's making charges and wastage deductions can swallow years of price gains.
  • Underkarat and fake gold: items sold as 22k that assay lower are a real problem. Hence certified jewellers, hallmarks, and receipts.
  • No inherent income: unleased physical gold pays no coupon or dividend. Alternative returns are variable and must be sourced separately.
  • Price volatility: international gold can fall hard and stay down. Buyers in strong-currency years have waited years to break even.
  • Storage and theft: physical holdings create custody risk. Funds replace personal custody with fund, trustee, counterparty and operational risks rather than removing risk entirely.

08The Tax Angle

Tax treatment can depend on the instrument, transaction, taxpayer and character of the gain. Use the current Income Tax Ordinance and FBR guidance; a physical sale, mutual-fund distribution or gain, and futures transaction need not be treated identically. Retain purchase, custody, fund and sale records.

Decision record

State which gold exposure is being evaluated, the observation date, total round-trip cost, custody or fund structure, tax assumption and loss scenario. Do not convert a past currency hedge into a guaranteed future relationship or a fixed allocation.

09Frequently Asked Questions

Is gold a good investment in Pakistan right now?
This page does not issue a current buy call. Gold has no guaranteed return or hedge relationship. Compare an executable quote, spread, custody or fund costs, tax, time horizon and adverse scenarios with dated alternatives.
How do 24-karat and 22-karat gold differ?
They differ in fineness and typical use. Compare verified gold content, making charges, premium, durability and written buy-back deductions for the exact item. A karat label alone does not establish value or authenticity.
Are there gold mutual funds in Pakistan?
MUFAP's industry table identifies current commodity schemes. Verify the AMC's SECP status and read the exact fund's offering document, benchmark, holdings, expenses, custody, Shariah report where relevant, and redemption terms.
Is gold better than National Savings certificates?
They have different cash flows, costs and risks. Compare the current CDNS product rules with gold's executable buy and sell quotes over a stated period, after costs and tax. Neither a headline rate nor a past currency move establishes which is better for a person.
Do I have to pay tax on gold profits in Pakistan?
Treatment depends on the instrument, transaction and taxpayer facts. Check the current Income Tax Ordinance and FBR guidance; do not assume physical gold, commodity-fund units and futures are taxed identically.
What is the difference between a tola and a gram of gold?
A tola is the traditional South Asian unit of weight used for gold and equals approximately 11.66 grams. Gold dealers in Pakistan often quote prices per tola, while international markets quote per gram or per troy ounce (about 31.1 grams). When comparing rates, convert everything to the same unit so you are comparing like with like, and check the day's published rates from a recognised jewellers' association.
Do I pay Zakat on gold I hold as an investment?
Under widely-followed Islamic rulings, gold held above the nisab threshold for a full lunar year is generally subject to Zakat, calculated on its market value. The nisab for gold is traditionally around 87.48 grams (about 7.5 tola), though scholars differ on how to treat jewellery in regular use. For your specific situation, confirm the current nisab value and method with a qualified scholar or your local Zakat authority.
How can I verify that physical gold is genuine and pure?
Look for a hallmark or assay stamp indicating the karat or fineness, and buy from established dealers who provide a proper receipt stating weight and purity. Bars and coins from recognised refiners often carry serial numbers and certificates. If you have doubts, an independent assay or testing service can confirm purity, which is worth doing before any significant purchase.
Can I invest in gold without holding it physically?
Yes. The Pakistan Mercantile Exchange (PMEX) offers gold-linked contracts, and some SECP-regulated commodity funds give exposure to gold prices without you storing metal yourself. These instruments remove storage and security concerns but introduce their own costs, account requirements and market risks. Review the contract specifications, fees and regulatory status on the relevant exchange or fund's official documents before opening an account.
Why does the local gold price in Pakistan differ from the international price?
Local rupee gold prices are driven mainly by two factors: the international gold price in US dollars and the PKR-to-USD exchange rate. When the rupee weakens, local prices can rise even if the dollar price of gold is flat. Import duties, dealer margins and local supply and demand also create differences between the international benchmark and the rate you pay at a Pakistani jeweller.
