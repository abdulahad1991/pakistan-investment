TikTok Integration

Daily Market Brief → TikTok

pakinvestlysis.com is a free educational site of daily-updated investing tools and guides for Pakistani savers. Each market session it auto-generates a short Daily Market Brief video (KSE-100, USD/PKR, gold, SBP policy rate, inflation).

This page is the OAuth redirect endpoint for our TikTok integration, which uses:

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic) — one-time authorization of our own brand TikTok account.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload) — sends the rendered brief to the authorized account for publishing.

We post only our own first-party educational videos, only to the single account that authorized the app.

If you reached this page during sign-in, the authorization code is in your browser's address bar — that completes the one-time setup. Nothing else is needed here.
