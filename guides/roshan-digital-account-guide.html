What Is the Roshan Digital Account?

The Roshan Digital Account (RDA) is a foreign-currency and PKR bank account launched by the State Bank of Pakistan in September 2020, as part of Pakistan's broader financial inclusion and remittance drive. It was designed from the ground up to solve a problem that has frustrated overseas Pakistanis for decades: the difficulty of investing back home from abroad. With the RDA, the entire account-opening and investment process is conducted online — no trip to Pakistan required, no local guarantor, no branch visit.

The account is available to any Pakistani citizen living abroad who holds a valid CNIC (Computerised National Identity Card) or NICOP (National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis). This includes dual nationals — if you hold British, American, Canadian, or any other passport alongside your Pakistani identity documents, you are fully eligible. The account can hold multiple currencies simultaneously: USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, CAD, AUD, and PKR.

Six major banks currently offer RDA services: HBL, MCB, UBL, Meezan Bank, Allied Bank, and Bank Al-Falah. Each bank runs its own portal and mobile app, but all operate under the same SBP regulatory framework, which means the core account features are standardised across institutions.

The most significant advantage of the RDA — the one that separates it from ordinary Pakistani bank accounts — is the unconditional repatriation right. Any funds in your RDA, whether principal or profit, can be transferred back to your overseas bank account at any time without prior SBP approval. This is an explicit guarantee under SBP's RDA circular and gives overseas investors genuine confidence that their money is not trapped. It was this guarantee, more than anything else, that drove over $7 billion in foreign-currency inflows into RDA accounts in the program's first three years.

Which Banks Offer RDA and How They Differ

All six RDA banks operate under the same SBP-mandated framework, so the core account features — foreign-currency holding, NPC access, repatriation rights — are identical across institutions. Where they differ is in customer service quality, mobile app experience, NPC profit rates (which vary slightly bank to bank), and geographic convenience. Choosing the right bank depends primarily on where you live and what kind of investor you are.

HBL is the natural first choice for most overseas Pakistanis because of its unmatched global presence. It operates international branches and representative offices in over 25 countries, including major Pakistani diaspora hubs in the UK, USA, UAE, and Saudi Arabia. Its HBL Connect app is well-regarded and the bank's NPC onboarding is streamlined for overseas users. If you have any in-person banking needs, HBL gives you the most options.

Meezan Bank is the go-to for investors who require Shariah-compliant products. Its RDA operates on a profit-sharing basis rather than interest, and all investments — including its NPC equivalent (Roshan Apna Kar) — are structured under Islamic finance principles. Meezan is also consistently cited by customers for fast digital onboarding, often completing KYC within 2–3 business days. MCB Bank has a strong footprint in the UAE and Saudi Arabia, making it a practical choice for Gulf-based Pakistanis who may want a Pakistani account linked to a bank that understands their local context. UBL has historically served the European diaspora well, particularly in the UK. Allied Bank and Bank Al-Falah are solid all-rounders with competitive NPC rates — worth checking if you are rate-shopping.

Step-by-Step: How to Open an RDA

1

Visit Your Chosen Bank's RDA Portal

Each bank has a dedicated RDA page on their website. Search for "[Bank Name] Roshan Digital Account" — for example "HBL Roshan Digital Account" or "Meezan Bank RDA". Always go directly to the official bank website, not a third-party link, to protect your personal data.

2

Complete the Online Application

Fill in your personal details, current country of residence, and source of funds declaration. You will need: your CNIC or NICOP number, a recent passport-size photograph, a copy of your valid passport, and proof of overseas residence — typically a utility bill or bank statement from your country of residence, dated within the last 3 months. The form takes approximately 20–30 minutes to complete.

3

Complete Video KYC

Most banks now offer video-based KYC (Know Your Customer) verification — a short video call where a bank representative verifies your identity by inspecting your CNIC/NICOP and passport on camera. This typically takes 10–15 minutes. Banks usually accommodate different time zones, so you can schedule a slot during your local daytime hours. Some banks (including Meezan) offer asynchronous video upload instead of a live call.

4

Account Activation (3–7 Business Days)

After successful KYC, the bank's compliance team reviews your application. Approval typically takes 3–7 business days. You receive your account number and login credentials for the bank's online portal by email or SMS. Some banks send a physical welcome kit to your overseas address.

5

Fund Your Account via SWIFT Transfer

Transfer foreign currency from your overseas bank account using the SWIFT details provided by your Pakistani bank. Most banks offer preferential exchange rates for RDA remittances to encourage inflows. Your funds are credited in the original foreign currency — there is no mandatory conversion to PKR unless you choose to convert.

Naya Pakistan Certificates (NPCs) — The Star Product

Naya Pakistan Certificates are the flagship investment product available exclusively through the RDA channel. They are sovereign instruments issued by the Government of Pakistan's Ministry of Finance, which means they carry the full credit of the federal government — the same credit backing Pakistan's international Eurobonds. For overseas Pakistanis who want a straightforward, high-yield government investment without equity market exposure, NPCs are the most compelling option the RDA program offers.

NPCs come in two broad variants: conventional (interest-based) and Islamic (profit-and-loss sharing, Shariah-compliant). Both variants offer the same tenors and currencies — the difference is in the underlying structure. Islamic NPCs are the appropriate choice for investors who avoid interest-based instruments on religious grounds. Meezan Bank and Bank Al-Falah are particularly strong in Islamic NPC issuance.

Available tenors are: 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 3 years, and 5 years. You can hold NPCs in multiple currencies: USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, CAD, AUD, and PKR. Foreign-currency NPCs earn profit in the same currency — no exchange rate risk on the principal or profit if you hold to maturity and repatriate in the original currency.

As of May 2026, indicative NPC profit rates are approximately 5.5–6.0% per annum for USD (1-year tenor), 4.5–5.0% for GBP, and 3.5–4.0% for EUR. PKR-denominated NPCs earn at rates equivalent to the Central Directorate of National Savings (CDNS) instruments. These rates vary by bank and are updated periodically — always confirm the current rate directly with your bank before opening, as they can shift with SBP policy decisions and global interest rate movements.

The tax treatment is the headline advantage: profit earned on NPCs by non-resident Pakistanis is fully exempt from Pakistani income tax under the Income Tax Ordinance 2001 (as amended). You receive 100% of the stated profit rate — there is no withholding tax deducted at source for NRP NPC holders.

To invest: log into your RDA bank's online portal, navigate to the Investments section, select "Naya Pakistan Certificates", choose your currency and tenor, enter the amount, and confirm. The process takes under five minutes once your RDA is funded. Profit is credited at the end of each tenor period (or semi-annually for longer tenors, depending on the bank). Both principal and profit are repatriable at maturity in the original foreign currency.

Investing in PSX Stocks via RDA

The RDA gives overseas Pakistanis access to the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) — one of Asia's highest-yielding markets historically. The process requires linking your RDA to a SECP-licensed brokerage account, which can be done entirely online. You do not need to be physically present in Pakistan, and you do not need a local contact to act on your behalf.

To get started, choose a PSX-licensed broker that accepts RDA-linked accounts. Arif Habib Securities, MCB-FSL (MCB Financial Services Limited), and Akseer Research are among the most commonly recommended by overseas investors, all SECP-licensed and offering online account opening for non-residents. Once your brokerage account is open, transfer PKR from your RDA to the brokerage via standard bank transfer. If your RDA is in USD or another foreign currency, your bank converts to PKR at the interbank rate at the time of transfer.

From the brokerage platform, you can buy and sell KSE-100 index shares, receive dividend payments, and monitor your portfolio in real time. Dividends are credited to your bank account registered with the Central Depository Company (CDC) — this must match your RDA-linked account. When you want to repatriate, simply sell your shares, receive PKR proceeds to your RDA-linked bank account, request a PKR-to-foreign-currency conversion through your RDA bank, and initiate a SWIFT transfer to your overseas account. The full chain — from equity sale to overseas receipt — typically completes within 5–7 business days.

One administrative note: when opening a brokerage account as a non-resident, you will be required to complete FATCA (Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act) and CRS (Common Reporting Standard) declarations, providing your country of residence and tax identification number. This is a legal requirement and is not optional.

Investing in Mutual Funds via RDA

Mutual funds are an excellent option for RDA investors who want Pakistan-market exposure without the complexity of stock selection. Pakistan's mutual fund industry, regulated by the SECP, offers a wide range of products — equity funds, money market funds, income funds, and Islamic alternatives — with professional management and daily liquidity.

The process mirrors the PSX route: transfer PKR from your RDA to your chosen Asset Management Company (AMC) account, invest in your selected funds, and redeem when ready. Most major AMCs now accept RDA-origin funds, though it is worth confirming eligibility before opening. Al Meezan Investment Management, NBP Funds, and JS Investments have confirmed RDA investor acceptance. To open an AMC account, you need your NICOP, a selfie, and your RDA-linked bank account details. The process is fully online and takes 1–2 business days for approval.

For overseas Pakistanis who prefer Shariah-compliant investing, Islamic mutual funds are a strong fit. Al Meezan — Pakistan's largest Islamic AMC — offers a full suite of Islamic equity, income, and money market funds that are accessible via RDA. Returns from Islamic funds are structured as profit-sharing distributions rather than interest, making them suitable for investors with religious objections to conventional finance.

When you redeem mutual fund units, the AMC processes the redemption within T+2 or T+3 business days (depending on the fund type). Proceeds are credited to your RDA-linked bank account. From there, you follow the same repatriation path as PSX investments: convert PKR to foreign currency via your RDA bank and initiate a SWIFT transfer. The entire process from redemption request to overseas receipt typically takes 7–10 business days.

Tax Treatment for Overseas Pakistanis

Understanding the Pakistani tax implications of RDA investments is essential — both to optimise your returns and to stay compliant. The good news is that NRPs enjoy several meaningful tax advantages that resident investors do not.

NPC profits are the clearest benefit: fully exempt from Pakistani income tax for non-resident account holders under the Income Tax Ordinance 2001. No withholding tax is deducted at source, and you do not need to declare NPC income in a Pakistani tax return to claim the exemption — it is automatic for NRP account holders.

PSX dividend income is subject to withholding tax (WHT) at the rate of 15% for filers and 30% for non-filers. Even as a non-resident living abroad, you benefit significantly from being on FBR's Active Taxpayer List (ATL). Filing a Pakistani income tax return as an NRP takes approximately 30 minutes on the FBR's IRIS portal and costs nothing — it is one of the most impactful 30 minutes you can spend as a Pakistani investor. Mutual fund dividend distributions carry the same 15%/30% WHT structure.

Capital gains on equity mutual funds held for more than 4 years are taxed at 0% CGT — the same rate as for resident Pakistanis. For shorter holding periods, standard CGT rates apply. Capital gains on PSX stocks are also subject to CGT based on the holding period.

Pakistan has double taxation avoidance treaties (DTAs) with several major diaspora countries including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, USA, Canada, and Australia. If you are resident in one of these countries, you may be able to claim a credit in your country of residence for taxes withheld in Pakistan, avoiding double taxation on the same income. Review your country's specific DTA with Pakistan — or consult a cross-border tax advisor — before making large investments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I open an RDA if I have dual nationality?
Yes. Roshan Digital Account is available to any Pakistani citizen holding a valid CNIC or NICOP (National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis), regardless of whether you hold dual nationality. Your NICOP number is used for identification. Non-Pakistani spouses of overseas Pakistanis cannot open an RDA directly but can be added as nominees.
How long does it take to open an RDA?
The online account opening process typically takes 20–30 minutes to complete the application. Bank verification and KYC review usually takes 3–7 business days. Once approved, you receive your account credentials and can immediately begin transferring foreign currency. Meezan Bank and HBL are known for faster onboarding.
Can I close my RDA and repatriate all my money?
Yes. RDA funds are fully repatriable — you can transfer both your principal and profits back to your foreign bank account at any time, without prior SBP approval. This is one of the most significant advantages of the RDA program and is guaranteed under SBP's RDA circular. There are no exit restrictions or lock-in periods for repatriation.
Are Naya Pakistan Certificate profits really tax-free?
Yes, for overseas Pakistanis (NRPs). Profit earned on NPCs by non-resident account holders is exempt from Pakistani income tax under the Income Tax Ordinance 2001 (as amended). This exemption applies only to the NPC profit — other income types (PSX dividends, mutual fund gains) may still attract withholding tax at applicable NRP rates.
Which bank is best for opening an RDA?
It depends on your location and priorities. HBL has the widest global presence and is best for Pakistanis in the UK, USA, and Gulf. Meezan Bank is best for Islamic account holders and offers fast onboarding. MCB is popular in the Middle East. For Pakistanis in Europe, UBL has a good presence. All six banks offer the same SBP-mandated RDA features — the difference is in the app quality and customer service.
