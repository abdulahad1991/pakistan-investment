TikTok Integration

Daily Market Brief → TikTok

pakinvestlysis.com is a free educational site of daily-updated investing tools for Pakistani savers. Each market session it auto-generates a short Daily Market Brief video (KSE-100, USD/PKR, gold, SBP policy rate, inflation) and posts it to our own brand TikTok account.

Connect the brand TikTok account once to authorize automated posting. We use:

  • 🔐
    user.info.basic — Login Kit Identify the account that authorized the app.
  • ⬆️
    video.upload — Content Posting API Upload the rendered brief to the account as a draft.
  • 📣
    video.publish — Content Posting API Publish the brief directly once the app is approved.
Public key — appears in the authorize URL. The client secret is never used here.
You'll be sent to TikTok to log in as the brand account and approve the scopes above, then returned to this page. We post only our own first-party educational videos, only to the single account that authorizes the app.

✅ Authorized

TikTok returned an authorization code to pakinvestlysis.com/tiktok. The refresh token is exchanged and stored server-side to post the Daily Market Brief automatically.

Setup complete — nothing else is needed on this page. The daily job now uploads each rendered brief to the authorized TikTok account via the Content Posting API.
