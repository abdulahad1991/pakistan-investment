Money market funds vs bank savings in Pakistan

By Abdul Ahad · Source-checked 21 August 2026 · 12 min read
Two different legal structures

A bank account is an eligible deposit with access terms set by the bank. A money market fund is an investment whose NAV, portfolio, fees and redemption terms are defined in its offering documents.

Quick answer: Compare a bank's posted deposit rate and access terms with a fund's latest MUFAP return, offering document, fees and redemption cut-off. Eligible bank deposits have DPC protection up to the current limit; mutual fund units do not. A fund's return and settlement time are not guaranteed.

  • Use the bank's current rate sheet, account terms and the DPC eligibility rules rather than the SBP policy rate alone

  • Confirm cut-off times, redemption settlement, tax treatment and the amount you may need immediately before choosing either structure

Bank deposits and money market funds are sometimes compared only by a headline percentage. That comparison is incomplete. The quoted period, tax basis, fees, access time and legal protection differ, and all of them can change the result.

This guide explains the structures and provides a worksheet for comparing them. It does not rank providers or tell you how much to place in either product. Current inputs must be taken from the linked regulator, bank and fund documents on the date of comparison.

01Why headline rates are not enough

A nominal return does not show the change in purchasing power. For a dated comparison, take the latest twelve-month CPI reading from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics CPI release and compare it with each product's return over the same period, after disclosed fees and applicable tax. Do not mix a one-day annualised yield with a trailing twelve-month return.

The SBP policy rate, a bank deposit rate and a fund return are related but not interchangeable. Bank pricing depends on account type and regulation. A fund's return depends on its actual holdings, duration, credit exposure and expenses. Record the source and observation date for every number used.

02Why your bank pays what it pays: MDR explained

SBP rules set minimum-return requirements for covered savings deposits, but the scope matters. BPRD Circular Letter No. 15 of 2026 limits the minimum-profit requirement from 1 August 2026 to deposits of natural persons with a monthly average balance up to PKR 10 million; it also refers readers to the existing conventional and Islamic instructions. This is not a universal rate for every account or balance.

Conventional and Islamic savings products operate under different return mechanisms. Avoid applying the conventional MDR formula to an Islamic deposit. Check the bank's current key fact statement and rate sheet, then confirm which SBP instruction applies to the account and balance being evaluated.

Term deposits, premium accounts and balances outside the rule's scope may have different rates and early-withdrawal conditions. The bank's own dated disclosure is therefore the comparison input; do not infer the account rate from the policy rate.

03What exactly is a money market fund?

A money market fund is a mutual fund overseen by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. It pools unit holders' money and invests according to the mandate in its offering document, commonly using short-dated government securities, bank placements and other permitted money-market instruments. Its unit price is a NAV, not a guaranteed deposit balance.

Key features for a retail saver:

  • NAV-based value. Units are priced at the NAV calculated under the fund documents; returns can be reflected in NAV or distributions.
  • Fund-specific risk. Read the stability rating, credit-quality breakdown, weighted average maturity and exposure limits. A rating is an opinion, not a guarantee.
  • Conventional and Islamic categories. Verify the category and Shariah governance in the offering document rather than relying on a fund name.
  • Variable return. Short-term market rates influence returns, but fees, holdings and valuation dates mean a fund will not mechanically equal the SBP policy rate.

Use MUFAP's daily performance table to compare funds within the same category and period. Then open each fund's factsheet or offering document to verify expenses, rating, portfolio, minimum subscription and redemption terms. MUFAP's table is a historical performance source, not a product endorsement. If its observation date is stale, postpone the numerical comparison.

05Deposit protection vs ring-fencing: know the difference

Eligible bank deposits have statutory protection. The Deposit Protection Corporation applies its current limit per eligible depositor per member bank if SBP declares a member bank failed. Coverage depends on the depositor and deposit type. Verify both the amount and eligibility in the DPC depositor FAQ; do not describe every bank balance as insured.

Mutual fund units are not deposits and have no DPC cover. Fund assets are held through a trustee structure described in the constitutive documents. That separation does not remove market, credit, liquidity, operational or valuation risk, and it does not guarantee a redemption value or settlement date. Read the fund's risk disclosures rather than treating ring-fencing as insurance.

06Build an access ladder

The required mix is personal. Map expected payment deadlines to the actual access promise of each product before comparing returns.

Questions to document
  • Immediate access: what amount could be needed outside business hours, and which account can make that payment?
  • Short-notice access: what does the fund document promise after weekends, holidays and cut-off times?
  • Longer horizon: what penalty, price risk or rate uncertainty applies if money is committed for a fixed period?

Model a delayed redemption and a bank outage as separate cases. Any amount that must be payable immediately should not depend on a settlement assumption that the product documents do not guarantee. There is no universal one-month or six-month allocation supported by the sources used here.

07Step-by-step: opening a money market fund account online

Onboarding requirements, processing times and account limits vary by AMC and customer. First confirm that the AMC appears in the SECP asset-management licensing directory and that the specific fund appears in MUFAP's industry data.

1

Identify the exact fund

Use MUFAP's industry table to confirm the category and performance date, then read the AMC's current offering document, factsheet and fee disclosure.

2

Start digital onboarding

Use only the licensed AMC's official app or web portal. The portal will list the identity, bank-account and source-of-income evidence required for your account type; requirements are not identical for every customer.

3

Compare account restrictions

If an AMC offers a simplified account, read its current transaction and balance limits before selecting it. Complete the required KYC and risk-profiling process; do not use another person's identity or bank account.

4

Fund the account

Follow the AMC's named-bank-account and payment instructions. Confirm the applicable NAV date, cut-off time and any front-end or back-end load before submitting a subscription.

5

Record the redemption terms

Write down the fund's cut-off, settlement schedule, holidays, daily limits and registered redemption account. Marketing phrases such as "instant" should be checked against the formal terms and service conditions.

Ask the AMC how it determines tax status and what statements it provides. For current rates and classifications, use the FBR Tax Year 2027 withholding card; the result can depend on the type of return and the investor's status.

08Risks and comparison limits

Money market funds are investments, not guaranteed savings balances. Check at least these four points:

  • NAV risk. Changes in market yields, credit quality, liquidity or valuation can reduce the NAV. Short maturity can reduce sensitivity but does not remove loss risk.
  • Fees and loads. Confirm whether published returns are after recurring expenses and whether any subscription or redemption charge applies.
  • Historical-period mismatch. A trailing return contains earlier market conditions. It is neither a promised future rate nor directly comparable with a newly quoted fixed rate unless periods and assumptions are aligned.
  • Tax classification. Bank profit, fund distributions and capital gains can fall under different rules. Calculate net outcomes only after confirming the current FBR provision and the investor's facts.
Reproducible comparison

Save the rate sheet, factsheet and tax source used in the calculation, together with their dates. Re-run the worksheet when any input changes. Do not convert a historical ranking into a forecast or an allocation recommendation.

09Frequently asked questions

Can a money market fund in Pakistan lose money?
Yes. Its value is based on the NAV and is not contractually guaranteed. Interest-rate, credit, liquidity, operational and valuation risks are described in the fund's offering document and factsheet.
Are money market funds covered by deposit protection like bank accounts?
No. DPC protection applies to eligible deposits at member banks under the DPC rules, up to the current limit per eligible depositor per bank. Mutual fund units are not deposits and have no DPC cover.
How quickly can I withdraw money from a money market fund?
There is no universal settlement time. Check the specific fund's offering document and AMC service terms for the cut-off, business-day convention, settlement schedule, limits and registered bank-account rules.
Are there Shariah-compliant money market funds?
MUFAP lists Islamic money market categories. Verify the exact fund's category, offering document, Shariah adviser or board report, portfolio and purification policy before drawing a conclusion.
How do policy-rate changes affect fund returns?
Short-term rates influence the pricing and reinvestment yield of money-market instruments, but the timing and size of the effect depend on the fund's holdings, maturity profile, credit exposure and expenses. A policy-rate move does not establish a guaranteed fund return.
How are money market fund returns taxed in Pakistan?
Returns from mutual funds are generally distributed as dividends, and the AMC typically deducts withholding tax at source before the payout reaches you, with the rate often differing for filers and non-filers. Bank profit is taxed separately under its own withholding regime. The exact rates and treatment change with each Finance Act, so confirm the current figures on fbr.gov.pk or with your AMC before assuming a net yield.
What is the minimum amount needed to start a money market fund?
Minimums are set by each AMC and are usually modest, with many funds opening accounts for a few thousand rupees and some allowing small recurring top-ups after that. There is no single industry-wide figure, so check the offering document or the AMC's website for the exact minimum on the specific fund you are looking at. This is generally lower than the lump sum a bank may require for a high-yield term deposit.
How do I open a money market fund account in Pakistan?
Confirm the AMC's licence in SECP's asset-management directory, identify the exact fund in MUFAP's data, and use the AMC's official onboarding channel. Required KYC evidence, limits, payment methods and processing time depend on the customer and account type.
Is a money market fund always better than a bank savings account?
No. The structures differ in access, DPC protection, NAV risk, fees, tax and return certainty. Compare the exact bank account and fund using disclosures from the same date and requirements specific to the person using them.
What is the difference between a money market fund and a bank fixed deposit?
A fixed deposit follows a bank contract with a stated term, rate and early-withdrawal rules. A money market fund issues units at NAV under its fund documents, with variable performance and fund-specific redemption terms. Compare the actual contracts rather than relying on those broad labels.
