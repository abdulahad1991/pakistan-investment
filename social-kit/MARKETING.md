# Pakinvestlysis — Marketing Strategy

> **USP: "Everything in 60 seconds."** Pakistani investing — National Savings,
> PSX, gold, tax, funds — explained fast, in Urdu and English, with free live tools.
> Educational, never advice.

## 1. Positioning
- **Promise:** any money question about Pakistan, answered in ~60 seconds — a video, a calculator result, a one-line summary.
- **Proof:** every blog has a 60s Urdu explainer; every tool gives an answer in one screen; daily live data (KSE-100, gold, rates).
- **Voice:** honest, plain, no hype. Built by a software engineer, not a "financial guru." Always show the "as of" date + "not advice."
- **Why we win:** competitors give walls of text or one-off calculators. We give *fast answers + live data + Urdu video*, free, no login.

## 2. Audience (from market data)
| Segment | Who | What they want | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gen Z (18–30)** | 41% of new PSX investors; mobile-first | "where do I even start", stocks vs gold vs crypto | short Urdu video, quizzes, backtester |
| **Salaried savers** | filers/about-to-file | tax cuts, take-home, SIPs, National Savings | tax calc, SIP calc, filer guide |
| **Overseas Pakistanis** | Gulf/UK/US | RDA, Naya Pakistan Certificates, remittance | RDA/NPC guides, gold |
| **Religious savers** | zakat/halal-conscious | zakat, halal funds, gold | zakat calc, halal guide |

## 3. The content engine = the 60s video
The Urdu explainer on every blog **is the social content**. One script → many posts:
1. Export the explainer audio + captions as a vertical 9:16 clip (Reels/Shorts/TikTok).
2. Post the same clip to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn.
3. Pull 1 stat from it → a still image + caption (carousel/tweet).
4. Link the full blog/tool in bio / first comment.

**Cadence (realistic, automatable):**
- Mon/Wed/Fri: one 60s explainer clip (rotates through blogs + new ones).
- Daily (weekday): the auto Daily Brief (already rendered) → Shorts + LinkedIn.
- Weekly: one tool spotlight (backtester / SIP / portfolio) + one "myth vs fact".

## 4. Channels & priority
1. **YouTube Shorts + TikTok + Instagram Reels** — the 60s Urdu clips. Highest fit for Gen Z. Native upload, captions burned in.
2. **LinkedIn** (company page) — English, salaried/professional angle, tool spotlights, daily brief.
3. **Facebook groups** — Pakistani investing/savings groups: answer first, link when genuinely useful.
4. **Reddit** — white-hat only (see social-kit/README). r/pakistanFIRE, r/PakStockExchange, r/pakistan. Value-first text posts; create r/pakinvestlysis as content home.
5. **X/Twitter** — one-stat threads, market-day reactions, charts.
6. **WhatsApp/Broadcast** — daily brief + tool links to a subscriber list.

## 5. Content pillars (rotate)
- **Explain it in 60s** — one concept per clip (what is a SIP, filer vs non-filer, gold vs savings).
- **Show the number** — a tool result as a visual ("Rs 10k/month for 10 yrs = Rs X").
- **Myth vs fact** — "high dividend = good? Not always."
- **This week in the market** — daily brief, KSE/gold/rate moves.
- **How-to** — open RDA, become a filer, start a SIP.
