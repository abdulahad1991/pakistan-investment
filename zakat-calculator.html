How Zakat is calculated

This tool implements a common arithmetic pattern: add the entered asset categories, subtract entered liabilities, compare the result with the selected nisab, then multiply by 2.5%. It does not determine whether a category qualifies, whether a liability is deductible, whether hawl has been met or which jurisprudential view controls.

The form uses a simplified set of categories often discussed in basic calculations. The two columns describe what this calculator includes or leaves out by default, not a universal ruling:

The tool subtracts the full liability number entered and labels the remainder net wealth. That does not establish that every entered debt is deductible. Long-term debt, shares, pensions, receivables, jointly held property and business assets can require a different treatment.

The nisab: gold vs silver

The form offers two metal-weight conventions and converts the selected one using the price per tola entered by the user:

  • Gold nisab: 87.48 grams (7.5 tola)
  • Silver nisab: 612.36 grams (52.5 tola)

The two selections can produce materially different thresholds. The calculator deliberately does not recommend one. Record the metal-price source and date, because an old or retail-incomparable rate changes the threshold.

The lunar year (hawl)

The calculator does not track a lunar year, changes around the threshold or a calculation anniversary. It only evaluates the values currently entered. Hawl treatment must therefore be established outside the tool before the arithmetic is relied upon.

A note for investors

Hold PSX shares, mutual funds or savings certificates? Market-linked holdings can move materially between two Zakat dates, so enter the value on the calculation date rather than last year's figure. Zakat is separate from income tax. Rulings can differ by asset and school of thought; consult a qualified scholar for a personal ruling.

To sum up: the tool calculates 2.5% of the entered net amount when that amount reaches the selected, user-priced metal threshold. It does not establish asset treatment, deductible liabilities, hawl or the applicable nisab view.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly does the result calculate?
It adds the six asset inputs, subtracts the liability input, and applies 2.5% only when the remainder reaches the selected metal-weight threshold at the entered price.
Does the tool choose gold or silver nisab?
No. It exposes both inputs because the selection can materially change the threshold. Choosing the applicable view is outside the calculator's scope.
Does it verify the lunar-year condition?
No. No dates or balance history are collected, so the tool cannot test hawl or changes around the threshold.
Are all debts deducted automatically?
The arithmetic subtracts whatever liability amount is entered. It does not decide whether that amount is deductible under the view being followed.
Does it account for bank deductions?
No. The calculator does not import bank records or determine how a statutory bank deduction affects the rest of a person's calculation.
