Privacy policy.

Calculator inputs stay in your browser and no account is required. The portfolio scratchpad can persist entries in your browser's local storage; the site does not send those entries to an application server.

Summary

Pakinvestlysis does not sell calculator inputs. Calculations run locally. Google Analytics measures site usage, and selected pages load Google AdSense code; those third-party services can use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

01

Who We Are

Pakinvestlysis (pakinvestlysis.com) publishes independent educational tools, guides and dated datasets about Pakistan's financial markets. The site is operated by Abdul Ahad, an independent developer based in Pakistan.

You can contact us at: abdulahad1991@gmail.com

02

Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to operate this service:

  • Calculator inputs: Amounts entered into calculators are processed by client-side JavaScript. The site does not include those values in its analytics events.
  • Browser storage: The portfolio scratchpad saves entered rows in local storage on that browser so they remain after a reload. Removing the rows or clearing site data deletes that local copy.
  • Usage analytics: Google Analytics 4 receives page and device information plus events such as scroll depth, outbound-link clicks and time-based page engagement. Do not put personal information in a URL you send through the contact channel. You can use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Hosting data: GitHub Pages and network providers may process technical request data such as IP address, browser information and referring URL under their own terms.
03

Google Analytics, AdSense & Cookies

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page views and interaction events. Analytics cookies such as _ga can distinguish browsers and sessions. See the "How Google uses data" disclosure for Google's processing details.

Selected long-form pages load Google AdSense code and may display an ad when Google fills a reserved placement. Google and its partners may use cookies or other identifiers to deliver, limit and measure ads, subject to consent and account settings.

You can manage Google advertising preferences through My Ad Center.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit the Google Privacy Policy.

04

How We Use Information

We use the limited information collected solely to:

  • Operate, debug and improve the site
  • Measure use and, where available, display programmatic advertisements
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our service
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information to third parties for marketing purposes.

05

Data Storage and Security

Calculations happen in client-side JavaScript and no account is required. Portfolio rows can remain in that browser's local storage, but the site does not provide an application server that receives or synchronises them. Analytics, advertising and hosting providers process their own technical data as described above.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which employs industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all connections.
