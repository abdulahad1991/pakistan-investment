FAQ

Pakistan investing questions

How much money do I need to start investing in Pakistan?
Some National Savings products start near PKR 500, while many mutual fund SIPs start around PKR 1,000. Your time horizon matters more than the minimum.
Are National Savings better than mutual funds?
National Savings offers government-backed income. Mutual funds offer liquidity and growth potential. Most balanced portfolios use both.
Can I invest in Shariah-compliant products?
Yes. Islamic mutual funds, sukuk and Shariah-screened stocks are available. Start with the halal investing guide.
Is PSX investing risky?
Yes. PSX stocks can fall sharply in the short term. Use equities only for money you can leave invested through a market cycle.
Can overseas Pakistanis invest?
Yes. Roshan Digital Account supports remote access to Naya Pakistan Certificates, PSX stocks and funds. Read the RDA guide.
How often are rates updated?
The site refreshes daily from SBP, CDNS, MUFAP and PSX sources. Verify final rates with the institution before acting.
