Why Investing Matters More Than Ever for Pakistanis in 2026

Pakistan's headline inflation ran at roughly 7% in early 2026, which means money sitting idle in a zero-interest wallet or an underperforming savings account loses real purchasing power every single month. Even with the SBP policy rate now at 11.5% — sharply lower than the 22% peak of 2023 — the environment still rewards investors who understand which instruments to use and when to use them.

Every investment decision in Pakistan ultimately traces back to three main pillars:

National Savings (CDNS) Government-backed certificates and accounts that carry zero credit risk. Ideal as the safe anchor of any portfolio, especially for retirees or conservative savers.
Mutual Funds (SECP-regulated) Professionally managed funds spanning money market, fixed income, and equity strategies. SECP oversight means investor protections are in place. Minimum investments start at just PKR 1,000.
PSX Dividend Stocks Blue-chip companies listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange have paid consistent dividends for decades. A well-chosen dividend portfolio can generate passive income that outpaces inflation over a full market cycle.

This site is built for a wide range of readers: the salaried professional making their first investment, the overseas Pakistani looking to deploy remittances productively through a Roshan Digital Account, and the experienced investor who wants a quick data-driven reference to benchmark their returns. Every guide is written from first-hand investing experience, not generic financial theory.

All return figures, policy rates, and National Savings profit rates shown across this site are updated daily from official SBP and CDNS sources, so you are always working with current numbers rather than stale textbook examples.
