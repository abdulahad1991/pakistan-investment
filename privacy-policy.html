Privacy policy.

The short version: all calculations happen in your browser, no account is required, and we never store your financial data. Effective immediately upon your use of this site - the detail is below.

Summary

Pakistan Investment Advisor does not sell your personal data. All calculations are performed locally in your browser. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage and Google AdSense to display advertisements; both may use cookies.

01

Who We Are

Pakistan Investment Advisor (pakinvestlysis.com) is a free educational tool that helps Pakistani investors compare National Savings schemes, mutual funds, and PSX stocks. The site is operated by Abdul Ahad, an independent developer based in Pakistan.

You can contact us at: abdulahad1991@gmail.com

02

Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to operate this service:

  • Investment amount you enter: Typed into the calculator on the home page. This data is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to our servers.
  • Usage analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 to collect aggregate, anonymised usage statistics - pages visited, approximate location (country/city level), device type, and how visitors found the site. This helps us understand which guides are useful. Google Analytics uses cookies; we do not receive any personally identifying information from it, and we have not enabled advertising features or data sharing beyond Google's defaults. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Technical data: Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, referring URL) may be retained by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) for up to 30 days for security purposes.
03

Google Analytics, AdSense & Cookies

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement of page views and aggregate audience statistics). Analytics cookies (e.g. _ga) distinguish anonymous visitors; they do not identify you personally. See the "How Google uses data" disclosure for details.

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to serve personalised ads based on your browsing history. Google's advertising partners may collect information about your visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit the Google Privacy Policy.

04

How We Use Information

We use the limited information collected solely to:

  • Operate and improve the investment calculator tool
  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our service
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information to third parties for marketing purposes.

05

Data Storage and Security

Since all investment calculations happen in your browser (client-side JavaScript), we do not store your investment amounts, financial preferences, or any personally identifiable information on our servers. No account is required to use this service.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which employs industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all connections.

06

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party site before providing them with personal information.

07
